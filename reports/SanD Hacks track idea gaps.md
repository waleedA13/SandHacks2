# Phone the people alerts leave behind

The strongest project for SanD Hacks Fall 2026 is a **multilingual AI phone agent that calls at-risk residents when their evacuation zone changes, confirms they heard, and turns their spoken reply into a triage list**. Two research threads reached this idea independently: the ElevenLabs thread called it "LlamaAlert" and the Best Overall thread called it "Knock Knock". The evidence behind it is concrete. During the January 2025 LA fires, alerts went out only in English and Spanish, and **all 17 Eaton Fire deaths were in western Altadena, where electronic evacuation orders arrived hours late** ([KPBS](https://www.kpbs.org/news/politics/2025/06/16/emergency-alerts-go-out-in-spanish-and-english-but-what-happens-if-you-speak-another-language) [V]; [NBC](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/national-international/17-deaths-reported-in-area-that-did-not-receive-alert-until-hours-after-eaton-fire/6118089/) [S]). It is also the only idea that can be entered in six or more tracks while each sponsor's product still does real work. Its demo is the most reliable "physical wow" a software team can get at a science-fair table: the judge's own phone rings. The second pick is **RecallSweep**, a tool that finds recalled infant products listed for sale near a ZIP code. It is the best fit for the largest cash prize (Bright Data, $1000 first) and has the clearest vulnerable user. Higher-variance alternatives are the **aphasia word-finding copilot**, the most novel gap but the hardest to demo in a noisy hall, and **Neon "Rewind"**, the best pure database play. The report covers 11 prize categories with 5 ideas each where the research supports that many. It names ideas the research killed and the competitors that killed them, and it lists what must be checked at check-in this morning. Every link carries the verification tag from the notes, and many key claims rest only on search snippets ([S]). Treat those as leads to click, not facts to quote on stage.

Some context applies to every track. The MLH prize page lists Gemini, Solana, Mastra, Tiger Data, Vultr, GoDaddy Registry, DEV Writing and the SpaceXSI raffle ([MLH SanD Hacks prizes](https://www.mlh.com/events/sand-hacks/prizes) [V]). **ElevenLabs, Bright Data and Neon do not appear on that page.** They are organizer or sponsor tracks, and the researchers could not confirm their rules because Devpost returned 403 ([Devpost](https://sand-hacks-26.devpost.com/) [X]). The brief's judging criteria (Idea / Demo / Technical Complexity / Completion) also could not be confirmed on any official page; the event site renders only a title without JavaScript ([event site](https://hackathon.csesatucsd.com) [V]). One discrepancy matters: MLH lists the Vultr prize as "Assorted Prizes", not a specific device.

## 1. Build the evacuation caller and stack it into seven tracks

### Why the evacuation caller beats Rewind, RecallSweep and the aphasia copilot

The scores below combine the notes' 1–5 ratings (Idea / Demo / Tech / Completion) with the number of tracks each idea credibly enters.

| Candidate | Idea | Demo | Tech | Completion | Credible tracks | Biggest risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Evacuation caller (Knock Knock / LlamaAlert)** | 5 | 5 | 4 | 3–4 | Overall, ElevenLabs, Bright Data, Neon, Gemini, Vultr, Mastra, GoDaddy, A-Team raffle | Twilio/carrier setup; partial gap (Watch Duty covers text) |
| RecallSweep | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | Bright Data, Gemini, Neon, Vultr, GoDaddy, A-Team raffle (Overall plausible) | Unverified Bright Data Facebook Marketplace scraper; a live listing has to exist |
| Aphasia copilot (TipOfTongue) | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | ElevenLabs, Gemini, Neon, Vultr, Overall | Noisy room breaks stall detection; never tested on real aphasic speech |
| Neon Rewind | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | Neon, Gemini, ElevenLabs (voice), Vultr | Reads as a developer tool; no vulnerable user for Overall |

The evacuation caller wins because it scores high on all four criteria at once and is the only idea that matches both traits of recent grand-prize winners covered in the self-critique: a vulnerable named user, and a demo the judge physically experiences. The Overall thread scored it 22 out of a possible 25, the highest of any idea, on the strength of the Eaton Fire alert reporting ([Yahoo/CNN](https://www.yahoo.com/news/la-fires-delayed-wildfire-evacuation-183334965.html) [S]; [CBS LA](https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/investigation-emergency-alert-system-delays-deaths-eaton-fire/) [S]). The ElevenLabs thread gave it the highest raw total of its five ideas (17). It ranked the aphasia copilot first anyway, on novelty alone, and said to swap them if the team fears the noisy room. **A science-fair hall is a noisy room, so take the swap.**

The convergence is evidence in itself. One thread came at the problem from language access: UCLA found **12,000+ Asian American residents in four evacuation zones needed language help** ([AAPI Equity Alliance on UCLA](https://aapiequityalliance.org/asian-communities-faced-language-barriers-during-l-a-wildfires-ucla-study-says/) [V]), and federal multilingual Wireless Emergency Alerts were delayed ([Rep. Barragán / LA Times](https://barragan.house.gov/2025/05/27/los-angeles-times-trumps-fcc-delays-multilingual-emergency-alerts-for-natural-disasters-sparking-concern-in-l-a/) [V]). The other came from alert latency and the AG's civil-rights probe into age and disability discrimination ([ClickOnDetroit/AP](https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/national/2026/02/12/california-announces-investigation-into-delayed-evacuation-orders-during-la-area-wildfire/) [S]). Both arrived at the same product: a phone call, in your language, that waits for an answer. The merged design keeps four things from the two versions. From LlamaAlert it keeps **registration by phone call**, **verbatim translation of official text** with no improvisation on life-safety facts, and escalation of unconfirmed residents to volunteers. From Knock Knock it keeps the **free-speech triage classification** (safe / needs ride / medical / no answer) feeding a ranked callback board.

The weaknesses are real. The gap is partial. Watch Duty already ships Spanish, Chinese, Vietnamese and Filipino, but as text and push alerts in a smartphone app ([PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/watch-duty-app-now-available-in-spanish-chinese-vietnamese-and-filipino-301939736.html) [V]). Genasys markets multi-language alerting, but its two-way chat is for agencies ([Genasys multichannel](https://www.genasys.com/genasys-protect/multichannel-alerting) [V]; [Genasys Protect](https://genasys.com/genasys-evac/) [S]). Whether Everbridge-style keypad polling already covers "confirm you heard" was never verified. The pitch therefore has to be "**voice, phone, confirmation and Q&A for people without smartphones**", not "multilingual alerts". The Bright Data role (scraping county evacuation pages) is defensible but thinner than RecallSweep's. If the team cares most about the $1000 Bright Data prize, RecallSweep is the better entry there. A single team should still enter the evacuation caller in Bright Data. Use **Neon or Tiger Data, not both**: the notes warn that two Postgres databases invite "why two?" in Q&A. Neon fits the subscriber and zone roster and is the organizer track. Tiger fits zone-status and call-event time series. Neither database track is won by this project, because Neon judges reward branching, which the caller does not need.

**RecallSweep is the second pick**: for a second team, or as the pivot if Twilio fails by 2pm Saturday. CPSC found "thousands" of recalled Boppy loungers on Facebook Marketplace, linked to **10 infant deaths**, while sending about **1,000 takedown requests a month** by hand ([Click2Houston/AP](https://www.click2houston.com/business/2023/06/06/recalled-pillows-linked-to-10-infant-deaths-still-being-sold-on-facebook-marketplace-us-agency-says/) [V]). The only existing tools match product rows you supply yourself ([CatalogRecall API](https://catalogrecall-api.pages.dev/) [V]). **Rewind** is the right pick only for a team that wants the Neon prize above all. The **aphasia copilot** is the right pick only for a team willing to accept completion risk in exchange for the most novel gap in the whole study.

## 2. Per-track ideas, evidence and stacking

Each idea below lists its pitch, its evidence, its competitors, why the sponsor's technology is core, its demo, its scores (Idea / Demo / Tech / Completion, 1–5, from the notes) and the tracks it stacks into.

### 2.1 Best Overall: three independent ideas survived, and two slots are filled by variants

The Overall thread kept three original ideas and admitted that its fourth and fifth slots are a cross-listed Vultr idea and a variant of its first. Stronger Overall substitutes from other tracks are noted at the end of this section.

**O1 Knock Knock (merged with LlamaAlert): an AI that phones every at-risk resident when an evacuation zone changes, and listens.** *Problem:* the alert failures above, plus a state civil-rights probe ([ClickOnDetroit/AP](https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/national/2026/02/12/california-announces-investigation-into-delayed-evacuation-orders-during-la-area-wildfire/) [S]) and an LA County Fire review finding a "series of weaknesses" ([Fire Engineering](https://www.fireengineering.com/firefighting/wildland-firefighting/eaton-fire-no-misconduct-delay-in-evacuation-notices-to-west-altadena-ca-report-says/) [S]). *Users:* senior centers, CERT teams and mutual-aid groups that keep rosters of elderly, disabled or limited-English residents and do not own Genasys. *Competitors:* Watch Duty (text and push in a smartphone app), Alert San Diego (an English/Spanish/Tagalog app: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id561733287) [V]), and Genasys (agency-side) all lack spoken confirmation and Q&A for people without smartphones. *Sponsor core:* ElevenAgents with Twilio batch outbound calling and 90+ language TTS/STT ([ElevenLabs docs overview](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview) [V]; [Agents docs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/agents) [V]). *Demo:* a judge registers in Korean, the team flips the zone, the judge's phone rings, the judge says "I use a wheelchair", and their row jumps to the top in red. *Scores:* 5/5/4/3 (22 with stacking). *Stacks:* ElevenLabs, Bright Data, Neon or Tiger, Gemini, Vultr, Mastra, GoDaddy, A-Team raffle.

**O2 Ten Percent (the same idea as Bright Data's GougeWatch): a rent-gouging watchdog that files the complaint.** *Problem:* volunteers flagged **1,343 Zillow listings in 11 days**, later tallies reached about 18,360 potential >10% hikes, and **charges followed for under 1%** ([LAist](https://laist.com/news/housing-homelessness/los-angeles-price-gouging-fires-housing-rent-brigade-spreadsheet-zillow-study) [V]; [The Real Deal](https://therealdeal.com/la/2026/02/12/evidence-of-post-wildfire-rent-gouging-leads-to-few-lawsuits) [S]; [NLIHC](https://nlihc.org/resource/report-finds-emergency-declaration-los-angeles-wildfires-was-followed-rent-gouging) [S]). *Competitor:* Rent Brigade's manual spreadsheet. *Demo:* a price-cliff chart for an LA ZIP across Jan 7, 2025, and a complaint PDF. *Scores:* 5/4/4/4 (21). *Stacks:* Bright Data, Tiger Data, Gemini, Neon, ElevenLabs. Full detail is under GougeWatch in 2.2. Prepare for the landlord counter-argument that prices rose before the fires ([AAGLA](https://members.aagla.org/news/debunking-the-myth-los-angeles-wildfires-and-rent-gouging-claims-lack-evidence) [S]).

**O3 Quiet Ledger, full stack: a DV evidence timeline on private inference, with a tamper-evident hash.** *Problem:* NNEDV's free DocuSAFE evidence app **stopped working Oct 1, 2023** ([techsafety.org](https://techsafety.org/docusafesurvivors) [S]), and court documentation is tedious ([NNEDV guidance PDF](https://www.techsafety.org/s/Considerations-for-Documenting-Abuse-in-Preparation-for-Court_Final.pdf) [S]). *Demo:* 12 screenshots become a dated incident table, then a one-pixel edit turns the integrity check red. *Scores:* 5/4/4/3 (20). *Stacks:* Vultr, Solana (hash anchoring), Neon, ElevenLabs, Mastra. Full detail is under V2.

**O4 Caption Debt as an Overall entry.** This is V1 (see 2.9) cross-listed. Its demo is a cost counter. *Scores:* 4/3/4/4 (19). *Stacks:* Vultr, ElevenLabs, Gemini, Neon.

**O5 Knock Knock: Heat.** This is the same engine as O1, triggered by excessive-heat warnings to call isolated elderly residents. **The notes gathered no heat-mortality evidence links**, so it is a variant, not a fifth idea. Do not pitch it without sourcing.

**Stronger Overall substitutes than O4 and O5:** RecallSweep (2.2 #1), the aphasia copilot (2.3 #1), RollMap (2.8 #1) and the Lab Technique Coach (2.6 #1). The last two have a physical object on the table, which matches the grand-prize pattern better than any Overall-thread idea except O1.

### 2.2 Bright Data "Data for Good": recalled baby gear beats rent gouging on demo and finishability

Bright Data's past public winners are price comparison, news and Reddit summarizers, company OSINT and learning plans ([DEV challenge winners](https://dev.to/devteam/congrats-to-the-bright-data-web-scraping-challenge-winners-46nf) [V]; [Reputato](https://dev.to/devteam/congratulations-to-the-winner-of-the-bright-data-real-time-ai-agents-challenge-h92) [S]; [Bright Initiative](https://brightinitiative.com/hackathons) [V]). None of them is an enforcement tool, so evidence packets for regulators are an open lane. All five ideas below need multi-platform, anti-bot, repeated crawling, which is what makes Bright Data load-bearing. The research did not verify whether ready-made Facebook Marketplace, Zillow or OfferUp scrapers exist in the Bright Data dashboard, and that one check decides whether #1 and #2 fit in 24 hours.

**#1 RecallSweep: find recalled baby and kids' gear for sale near you, and report it.** *Problem:* thousands of recalled Boppy loungers on Facebook Marketplace, 10 deaths, more than 3M units recalled, and Commissioner Trumka saying Meta "has not taken effective action" ([Click2Houston/AP](https://www.click2houston.com/business/2023/06/06/recalled-pillows-linked-to-10-infant-deaths-still-being-sold-on-facebook-marketplace-us-agency-says/) [V]). A CPSC team issues about 55,000 takedowns a year by hand ([Sinclair](https://newschannel9.com/news/nation-world/hidden-danger-recalled-products-resold-online-facebook-marketplace-craigslist-ebay-liability-rock-n-play-baby-death-reported-newborn-infant) [S]), and lawmakers tie recalled products still on Marketplace to more than 100 infant deaths ([FOX 5 DC](https://www.fox5dc.com/news/recalled-products-tied-to-infant-deaths-sold-meta-facebook-marketplace) [S]). Ground-truth recall data is free ([CPSC recalls API](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/CPSC-Recalls-Application-Program-Interface-API-Information) [V]). *Competitors:* CatalogRecall matches only rows you supply ([CatalogRecall](https://catalogrecall-api.pages.dev/) [V]). An Apify "Marketplace Watch" actor reads only CPSC records, despite its name ([Apify actor](https://apify.com/taroyamada/cpsc-product-recall-marketplace-watch-scraper) [V]). *Sponsor core:* Facebook Marketplace and OfferUp block bots, eBay and Craigslist need geo-distributed requests, and recall checks must be continuous. *Demo:* type "92093" and a map shows a real recalled Rock 'n Play listed in San Diego this week, next to the CPSC photo, with a generated report packet. *Scores:* 5/5/4/4. *Stacks:* Gemini (vision matching), Neon, Vultr, optional ElevenLabs. *Legal:* Meta v. Bright Data favored logged-out scraping ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/24/court-rules-in-favor-of-a-web-scraper-bright-data-which-meta-had-used-and-then-sued) [V]), but commentators call the ruling narrow ([Quinn Emanuel](https://www.quinnemanuel.com/the-firm/news-events/client-alert-what-does-the-meta-v-bright-data-summary-judgment-ruling-mean-for-web-scraping/) [S]). Never log in, and pre-run the crawl Saturday night so cached hits exist.

**#2 GougeWatch: a disaster declaration starts price-gouging monitoring automatically.** *Problem:* 1,343 flagged Zillow listings in 11 days, an estimated $7.7M a month in overcharges, and a 46% median increase in the cheapest quartile ([LAist](https://laist.com/news/housing-homelessness/los-angeles-price-gouging-fires-housing-rent-brigade-spreadsheet-zillow-study) [V]). The count later grew past 8,000, and AG Bonta filed charges ([PBS SoCal](https://pbssocal.org/news-community/how-widespread-is-post-fire-rent-gouging-these-volunteer-sleuths-analyzed-the-data) [S]; [Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2025/05/la-rent-gouging-fires-housing-calif/) [S]). The tracker itself admits Zillow misses rentals and Airbnb history is hard ([Torched.la](https://www.torched.la/the-spreadsheet-brigade-thats-keeping-las-rental-market-from-exploding/) [V]). OpenFEMA provides a machine-readable trigger ([OpenFEMA](https://www.fema.gov/about/openfema/data-sets) [V]). *Competitors:* Rent Brigade (LA-only, manual). TruthCart and PriceGhost cover e-commerce pricing with no disaster trigger ([TruthCart](https://lablab.ai/submissions/rezzxaptfrvym2ow1fx4oan4) [S, redirected, possibly dead]; [PriceGhost](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/brightdata-ai-agents-web-data-hackathon/team-priceghost/priceghost-dynamic-pricing-forensic-expose) [S]). *Demo:* replay the Jan 7, 2025 declaration and reproduce in minutes what volunteers did in about 10 days. *Scores:* 5/4/5/3. *Stacks:* Tiger Data, Gemini, Neon, Vultr, ElevenLabs. Ship California rules only; the PIRG 37-state count may be stale ([PIRG](https://pirg.org/edfund/resources/how-report-price-gouging-after-natural-disaster-2/) [S, 403]).

**#3 VoucherWatch: a continuous "No Section 8" ad monitor that drafts complaint packets.** *Problem:* a study of more than 1M Craigslist ads found blatant source-of-income discrimination in about 10% of listings in some cities, and five of the ten worst cities ban it ([Northeastern](https://news.northeastern.edu/2022/08/17/discrimination-on-craigslist) [V]). Violations "remain commonplace" in California four years after SB 329 ([I've Ben Thinking](https://ivebenthinking.substack.com/p/illegal-section-8-discrimination-california) [V]). The DC AG has sued over such ads ([DC OAG](https://oag.dc.gov/node/1832) [S]). *Competitors:* the Northeastern tool was never released, and Zillow's Fair Housing Classifier screens chat questions, not ads on other sites ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/pro/tech-policy/2024/05/21/zillow-releases-tech-for-fair-housing) [S]). *Demo:* a live count of illegal San Diego ads this week, one of them opened, and its complaint draft. *Scores:* 4/4/3/5. *Stacks:* Gemini, Neon, Vultr. Add LLM detection of coded language ("no programs") to show technical depth. Present output "for advocate review", never as public shaming, because of defamation risk.

**#4 RentReal: a cross-platform rental-scam checker.** *Problem:* about **65,000 FTC rental-scam reports and $65M lost**, half starting with a fake Facebook ad, and **18–29-year-olds three times likelier to lose money** ([FTC Data Spotlight](https://ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2025/12/rental-scams-hit-home-65-million-reported-losses) [V]). *Competitor:* Bitdefender Scamio analyzes messages and links but does not cross-match listings ([Bitdefender](https://bitdefender.com/en-us/blog/hotforsecurity/rental-scams-fake-rental-listings/) [S]). *Demo:* paste a captured scam-style listing and the tool finds the original Zillow listing with a different agent. *Scores:* 4/3/4/3. *Stacks:* Gemini, ElevenLabs, Neon. It must never say "safe", only "no red flags found".

**#5 PantryPulse: freshness checks for food-pantry listings.** *Problem:* only about half of 50 Bronx pantries were open at their listed hours ([Medical Xpress](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2018-07-urban-food-pantries-unreliable-resource.html) [V]). *Competitor:* findhelp.org lists pantries but describes no freshness process ([findhelp](https://www.findhelp.org/) [V]). *Scores:* 4/3/3/4. *Stacks:* ElevenLabs (a voice agent that calls pantries to confirm hours), Gemini, Neon. It ranks last because pantry websites do not need Bright Data.

### 2.3 ElevenLabs: phone calls beat laptop microphones, and healthcare agents are repeat territory

ElevenLabs' current platform includes Eleven v4/v4 Turbo TTS, Scribe v3 Live real-time STT in 90+ languages, and ElevenAgents with server tools, MCP, Twilio/SIP and batch outbound calling ([docs overview](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview) [V]; [agent tools](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-agents/customization/tools.md) [V]). ElevenLabs publishes its own hackathon winners, so its judges know the repeats: GibberLink and voice assistants ([winners post](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/announcing-the-winners-of-the-elevenlabs-worldwide-hackathon) [V]), and Parkinson's screening, clinician handoff, AAC interpretation and multilingual NHS agents ([care-accessibility blog](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/how-hackathon-teams-are-using-elevenlabs-to-make-care-more-accessible) [V]).

**#1 TipOfTongue: a real-time aphasia word-finding copilot.** *Problem:* about **2 million Americans have aphasia** ([National Aphasia Association](https://aphasia.org/faq-list/how-common-is-aphasia/) [V]). An LLM recovered the intended word from circumlocutions **91.67%** of the time, and the authors note no program targets those moments ([Purohit et al. 2023](https://aphasia.talkbank.org/publications/2023/Purohit23.pdf) [V]). Researchers list real-time operation as future work ([Groningen](https://www.rug.nl/news/2023/10/when-you-re-constantly-looking-for-words) [V]; [Adikari 2025](https://talkbank.org/aphasia/publications/2025/Adikari25.pdf) [V]; [arXiv 2504.09435](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.09435v1) [V]). *Competitors:* Constant Therapy is offline exercises ([Constant Therapy](https://constanttherapy.com/) [V]). Voiceitt recognizes atypical articulation, not missing words ([Voiceitt](https://www.voiceitt.com/) [V]). Lingraphica and the "Word??" app could not be verified ([Lingraphica](https://www.lingraphica.com/) [UNVERIFIED]). *Sponsor core:* Scribe v3 Live with keyterm prompting from a personal vocabulary, low-latency TTS, and the user's own cloned voice. The Impact Program already licenses voices for people with speech loss, so restoring a voice is not the gap; what the user says with it is ([Impact Program](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/expanding-access-patients-and-clinicians-can-now-apply-directly-on-the-elevenlabs-website) [V]). *Demo:* "I need more of my… the… for blood, thin…" produces Eliquis / warfarin / aspirin tiles; tap one and it is spoken in the user's voice. *Scores:* 5/4/4/3. *Stacks:* Gemini, Neon, Vultr. Use a lapel mic and a push-to-hold fallback trigger.

**#2 LlamaAlert: a phone-first multilingual emergency line.** This is merged into O1; see 1 and 2.1. *Scores:* 4/5/4/4 (17, the highest raw total in this track). Its best stacking is Bright Data, Neon, Gemini and Vultr.

**#3 Ensayo: a SNAP/Medi-Cal interview rehearsal line.** *Problem:* **over 40% of Missouri's rejected SNAP applications in 2024** were for not completing the interview ([CDT](https://cdt.org/?p=119546) [403-bot]). 28% of US adults are at PIAAC Level 1 or below, and speech entry is about 3x faster than typing ([Propel](https://www.propel.app/insights/voice-by-default/) [V]). *Competitors:* GetCalFresh is web and text ([GetCalFresh](https://www.getcalfresh.org/) [V]). Josef/Frontline Q serves justice workers, not applicants ([LawNext](https://lawnext.com/2026/07/frontline-justice-and-josef-partner-on-multi-state-rollout-of-ai-to-protect-access-to-snap-benefits.html) [V]). Careforce runs renewal outreach ([CHCF](https://www.chcf.org/resource/how-ai-powered-outreach-is-helping-californians-keep-their-medi-cal-coverage/) [403-bot]). Polimorphic is agency-side ([Polimorphic](https://www.polimorphic.com/departments/more-accessible-health-and-human-services-with-ai) [V]). *Demo:* "I get paid in Venmo sometimes" adds "Venmo deposit screenshots" to a checklist that arrives by SMS. *Scores:* 4/4/3/5. *Stacks:* Gemini, Neon, Bright Data (scraping CalFresh document lists), Vultr. It prepares applicants only and never determines eligibility.

**#4 Sombra: heat check-in calls for farm crews.** *Problem:* crop workers' heat-death rate was about **20x** that of all workers, but the data covers 1992–2006 ([CDC MMWR](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/mm5724a1.htm) [403-bot]). A research team ran check-in calls by hand ([PMC9476265](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9476265) [V]). *Competitors:* the OSHA-NIOSH app gives information only ([OSHA](https://www.osha.gov/heat/heat-app) [V]). ### 2.4 Neon: the missing row-level diff and merge is the gap to build

Neon now offers branching, snapshots, Time Travel, instant restore, Neon Auth, the Data API, Functions and an MCP server ([branching for agents](https://neon.com/branching/branching-for-agents) [V]; [roadmap](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/roadmap) [V]). It has **schema diff but no row diff and no merge**; its own BranchDiff component says row changes have no endpoint ([Neon UI branch-diff](https://ui.neon.com/database/branch-diff) [V]; [schema diff](https://neon.com/docs/guides/schema-diff) [V]). The only verifiable past Neon contest was won by starter kits ([DEV Neon winners](https://dev.to/devteam/congrats-to-the-neon-open-source-starter-kit-challenge-winners-247p) [V]), so a project that breaks without branching should stand out. Instant restore works only on root branches ([instant restore](https://neon.com/docs/guides/branch-restore) [V]).

**#1 Rewind: Ctrl-Z and data-diff approval for business agents.** *Problem:* a Cursor agent wiped PocketOS's production database and backups **in 9 seconds** ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/it-took-9-seconds-ai-agent-running-on-anthropics-claude-opus-46-wipes-critical-database-527552-2026-04-27) [V]; [ITPro](https://www.itpro.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/nine-seconds-was-all-it-took-for-an-ai-agent-to-wipe-a-startups-database-experts-warn-its-a-glimpse-into-the-future-challenges-of-identity-security) [V]). A Replit agent deleted SaaStr's live database ([eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/replit-ai-coding-assistant-failure/) [V]). LangGraph's time travel explicitly does not undo database writes ([LangChain docs](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/langgraph/use-time-travel) [V]). *Competitors:* Dolt has data diff and merge but is MySQL and developer-facing ([DoltHub](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2025-10-30-karpathy-agents-need-diffs/) [V]). Xata branches and deletes with no merge or review ([Xata](https://xata.io/blog/every-ai-agent-needs-a-database-it-can-break) [V]). Replit App History is Replit-only ([Neon blog](https://neon.com/blog/replit-app-history-powered-by-neon-branches) [V]). Tiger Ghost is infrastructure ([SD Times](https://sdtimes.com/data/tiger-data-launches-ghost-a-purpose-built-database-service-for-the-agentic-er/) [V]). *Sponsor core:* a branch per action, server-side `EXCEPT` row diffs, replay on approve, and rewind via LSN or instant restore. *Demo:* "delete customers who haven't ordered this year" deletes 12 VIPs; the red diff shows it, the user rejects, and main is untouched. *Scores:* 5/5/4/4 (18). *Stacks:* ElevenLabs (spoken diff summary), Gemini, Vultr. "Merge" is really replaying recorded SQL, so check that the parent LSN is unchanged before replay.

**#2 Flight Recorder: the black box for agent decisions.** Each tool call is logged with `pg_current_wal_lsn()`, and a "why did it do that?" view runs a Time Travel query at that LSN ([Time Travel](https://neon.com/docs/guides/time-travel-assist) [V]). Memory layers cover memory facts only, and Mem0 can silently delete a memory ([Hightower](https://rickhigh.substack.com/p/eight-agent-memory-systems-none-of) [V]; [DEV](https://dev.to/mukesh_13/mem0-auto-resolves-memory-conflicts-for-you-until-it-silently-deletes-one-you-still-need-4f4m) [V]). *Demo:* a $500 refund traced to a policy row that read "$1000" at decision time. *Scores:* 4/4/4/4. *Stacks:* ElevenLabs, Gemini, Bright Data. **The notes recommend merging this into Rewind as its History tab.**

**#3 Crash-Test Lab: run your agent on anonymized forks before granting write access.** Research benchmarks score agents by database-state diff ([Agent-Diff](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.11224) [V]; [DBA-Bench](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2607.22165.md) [V]; [WorkBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00823) [V]), but nobody runs them on your own data. *Demo:* a leaderboard of 3 agents across 3 scenarios with a "rows destroyed" counter. *Scores:* 4/4/5/3. *Stacks:* Gemini, Bright Data, Vultr. Anonymized branches may be Console-only, so check API availability first.

**#4 Multiverse: parallel what-if branches, keep the best universe.** The supporting evidence is a tutorial ([MindStudio](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/ai-agent-isolated-database-environments-fork-experiment) [V]) and a consultancy post ([Syren](https://syrencloud.com/databricks-lakebase-ai-pricing-agent/) [403-bot]). *Demo:* four food-budget universes for 400 hackers. *Scores:* 3/5/4/4. It has the weakest problem evidence, and judges will ask "why a database branch?"

**#5 Forkable Study Buddy: a per-student database and memory you can rewind.** *Scores:* 3/3/3/5. Branching feels bolted on and AI tutors are plentiful. It is the safe fallback only.

### 2.10 GoDaddy Registry domain: name strategies, not products

### The survivors' weak points are legal framing and live-data dependence

The surviving ideas fail in three recurring ways. The first is **liability framing**. The evacuation caller (translation errors, TCPA consent), Safe Intake (unauthorized practice of law), Quiet Ledger (admissibility), Ensayo (eligibility advice) and VoucherWatch (defamation) each need a one-sentence answer for Q&A. In every case the answer is the same: opt-in, human-in-the-loop, "flag for review". The second is **dependence on live data that may not exist on Saturday**. RecallSweep needs a live listing, GougeWatch and ShiftSafe need replayed disasters, and GhostBus has only about 20 hours of collected data. The third is **sponsor-tech that is present but not essential**: Neon in Knock Knock, Bright Data in PantryPulse, and Solana in OpenPool's payout. Judges for those tracks will ask whether removing the product breaks the project.

On whether better ideas exist: the research did not surface a categorically stronger concept than the evacuation caller. The one thing it beats is the grand-prize pattern, which it matches only by substitution. **Every top idea is software-only**, and the two ideas with a real object on the table (RollMap's rolling chair, Lab Coach's titration) scored lower on stacking. A hardware-flavored version of a top idea could raise the Overall ceiling. An example would be a thrift-store countertop camera that runs RecallSweep's vision match on items as they are priced. That is this report's untested hypothesis, not something the research checked for competitors.

### Recent grand-prize winners pair a physical demo with a vulnerable user

Verified recent grand prizes are a motorized vision cane for blind users ([Stanford Daily, TreeHacks 2026](https://stanforddaily.com/2026/02/15/12th-annual-treehacks/) [V]), real-time AI video alerts for life-threatening events ([Stanford Daily, TreeHacks 2025](https://stanforddaily.com/2025/02/18/treehacks-awards-200000-in-prizes-to-students-from-around-the-world/) [V]), an EEG headset that generates music ([Stanford Daily, Cal Hacks 11.0](https://stanforddaily.com/2024/11/20/stanford-students-win-cal-hacks/) [V]), and an **under-$30 phone-attached pill tester** with an AI evidence engine ([RIT News, HackMIT 2026](https://www.rit.edu/news/rit-student-wins-hackmit-low-cost-medicine-testing-device) [V]). Four traits recur. Judges can touch the artifact or watch it react. A specific vulnerable user is named. AI is one component grounded in real data rather than a chatbot. The winners also took a sponsor track (Shepherd took fetch.ai and Peel took Elastic). A one-line cost or access hook ("under $30") helps. The evacuation caller hits the vulnerable user, the real-data grounding, the sponsor stacking and the access hook ("works on a flip phone"). It substitutes a ringing phone for hardware. Cal Hacks 12.0, LA Hacks and PennApps 2025 winners were not confirmed, so the pattern rests on four events.

## 5. Link verification legend

The notes used different tag spellings. This report normalizes them as follows. **[V]** = the page loaded during research (the notes' [V], [F] and [OK], plus untagged links in the ElevenLabs and Neon notes, which those notes state returned HTTP 200). "[V, title only]" or "[V, near-empty]" means the page loaded but showed little content. **[S]** = seen in live search results only; the page was not fetched, so treat it as unverified. **[403-bot]** = the site refused the automated fetch but appeared in search with matching content; it is probably live but not machine-verified. **[429]** = rate-limited (solana.com) and not load-verified. **[X]** = the fetch failed (403 or a redirect). **[UNVERIFIED]** = 404 or timeout; kept for context only, never cite it in a pitch. Where two notes tagged the same URL differently, this report uses the stronger tag (for example, LAist was [V] in the Bright Data notes and [S] in the Overall notes). No URL here comes from outside the research notes. Claims the notes recorded without a URL (LovShield, Relay TeamTranslate, the Dhukuti Protocol, Onerent, the qForge grand prize, Labster, and the Earthquake Brace + Bolt details) were left out or flagged as unsourced.

## Conclusion

Voice and telephony carry this event's best idea more than any other sponsor technology. The convergent idea did not come from looking for an ElevenLabs project. It came from asking who official alerts fail. The answer was elders without smartphones and without English, and a phone call is the only channel that reaches them and can also hear back. That points to a broader lesson for picking hackathon projects. The highest-scoring ideas across all threads (the evacuation caller, RecallSweep, Rewind, RollMap) each pair a documented, dated failure with a concrete number (17 deaths, 10 deaths, 9 seconds, research-only status) and a tool that turns detection into an action a regulator, volunteer or user can take. Ideas that only detected or only summarized died in critique.

The main uncertainty is not the idea but the morning's checks. Twilio setup, Bright Data scraper availability, SAS on devnet and the GoDaddy TLD list decide which plan is feasible, and all of them are 10-minute checks. Run them before writing code. Hold to the 2pm go/no-go on the phone call, and record the backup video before the space closes at 9pm.
